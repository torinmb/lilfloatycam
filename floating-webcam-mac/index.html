Floating webcam for Mac

A floating webcam for your Mac, without the box

Lil Floaty Cam puts your webcam in a window that floats above every app on your Mac, even full-screen ones. Turn on background removal and it's just you, cut out and floating over your work. No green screen, no extra software, no editing.

What it does

  • Floats over everything. Your camera stays on top of every window and every app, including full-screen apps and presentations.
  • Removes your background live. No green screen. It runs entirely on your Mac, so your video never leaves it.
  • Stays out of your way. Clicks pass straight through the space around you, and it can hide itself when your mouse gets close.
  • Looks the way you want. A clean cutout, or a classic camera bubble with any shape from square to a perfect circle.
  • Works with your recorder. Anything that records or shares your screen picks it up, so you're already in the shot.

Set it up in a minute

  1. Open Lil Floaty Cam. It lives in your menu bar.
  2. Choose your camera: the built-in FaceTime camera, a USB webcam or your iPhone.
  3. Turn on Remove Background for a cutout, or leave it off for a camera bubble.
  4. Drag yourself into place and resize from any corner.
  5. Start your screen recorder, video call or presentation. For a clean recording, turn on Streaming Mode to hide every border and handle.

Questions

How do I make my webcam float on top of other windows on a Mac?

Open Lil Floaty Cam. Your camera appears in a small window that stays above every app, including full-screen ones. Drag it where you want it and resize it from any corner.

Can I remove my webcam background on a Mac without a green screen?

Yes. Turn on Remove Background and Lil Floaty Cam cuts you out of your background in real time using your Mac's built-in machine learning. It works in a normal room with a normal webcam.

Will the floating webcam show up in my screen recording?

Yes. It's a regular window over your screen, so QuickTime Player, the screenshot toolbar (Shift-Command-5), OBS, ScreenFlow and screen sharing in Zoom, Meet or Teams all capture it.

Can I still click on things behind my webcam?

Yes. With Remove Background on, only your cutout catches clicks; everything around you clicks through to the app underneath. Hide Camera on Mouse Over hides you completely when your cursor comes close.

Does it work with my iPhone camera?

Yes. Pick your iPhone from the Camera menu to use it with Continuity Camera, or use the built-in FaceTime camera or any USB webcam.
