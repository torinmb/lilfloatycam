What it does

  • Removes your background live. Runs entirely on your Mac, so your video never leaves it.
  • Floats over everything. Your cutout stays on top of every app, including full-screen apps and presentations.
  • Clicks pass right through. Keep working in the app behind you while you record.
  • Works with any recorder. QuickTime, the screenshot toolbar (Shift-Command-5), OBS, ScreenFlow, and screen sharing in Zoom, Meet or Teams.

Questions

What is Lil Floaty Cam?

A lightweight native Mac app that removes your webcam background in real time and floats your cutout over any app, even full screen. Anything that records or shares your screen picks it up.

Do I need a green screen?

No. Background removal runs on your Mac using its built-in machine learning, in a normal room with a normal webcam.

How much does it cost?

$3.99 once, with free lifetime updates and no subscription. It's free for members on a paid Patreon tier.
