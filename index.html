How it works

One screen recording. You’re already in it.

Skip the second camera track and the compositing in post. Float yourself over your work and record it all at once. Step-by-step guide →

  1. 1

    Open Lil Floaty Cam

    It lives in your menu bar. Pick any camera, including your iPhone with Continuity Camera.

  2. 2

    Float anywhere

    Drag, resize and round the corners. You stay on top of every app, even full‑screen ones.

  3. 3

    Click right through

    Turn on Remove Background and everything around your cutout is see-through. Keep clicking, scrolling and typing in the app behind you.

  4. 4

    Hit record

    Use QuickTime, OBS, the screenshot toolbar or a video call. You’re already in the shot, so there’s nothing to edit.

FAQ

Before you download

Do I need a green screen?

No. Lil Floaty Cam removes your background in real time using your Mac's built-in machine learning. It works in a normal room with a normal webcam.

Which screen recorders does it work with?

All of them. Lil Floaty Cam is a regular window floating above your apps, so anything that records or shares your screen picks it up: QuickTime Player, the macOS screenshot toolbar, OBS, ScreenFlow, and screen sharing in Zoom, Meet or Teams.

Can I use my iPhone as the camera?

Yes. Lil Floaty Cam works with any camera your Mac sees, including the built-in FaceTime camera, USB webcams, and your iPhone with Continuity Camera.

Which Macs are supported?

Any Mac running macOS 14 Sonoma or later. It runs natively on both Apple silicon and Intel Macs.

Is it a subscription?

No. Lil Floaty Cam is a one-time $3.99 purchase with free lifetime updates. It's also free for members on a paid Patreon tier.

Is my video uploaded anywhere?

Never. Background removal runs entirely on your Mac. There's no account, no cloud processing, no analytics and no tracking.

How is this different from Loom?

Loom records and uploads your video for you. Lil Floaty Cam does one thing: it floats your camera, with the background removed, over your screen. Record with whatever tool you already like, and keep your files on your Mac.
